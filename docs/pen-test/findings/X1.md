# X1: resource exhaustion — findings

**Scope issue:** #69 · **Date:** 25 November 2026 · **Status:** done; findings fixed and verified 25 November 2026, in-process and, from 26 November 2026, by `x1-limits.probe.ts` in both configurations (its setup was fixed: see the H9 rows).

Hypotheses: [H3](../../product-requirements-document/pen-testing.md#h3-parallel-sign-ins-pass-the-limiter-and-each-costs-19-mib--high) (re-measured after #81 and #82), [H9](../../product-requirements-document/pen-testing.md#h9-a-hostile-container-exhausts-lookouts-memory-through-its-logs--medium). Probe tests: [`apps/server/src/pen-test/probes/x1-limits.test.ts`](../../../apps/server/src/pen-test/probes/x1-limits.test.ts), which runs in `pnpm test`, and [`x1-limits.probe.ts`](../../../apps/server/src/pen-test/probes/x1-limits.probe.ts) against the deployment (`pnpm pen-test:probe`); each deployment `today_…` probe's `it.fails` partner is the in-process one. Checks that apply: "Nothing is left to a collaborator to bound" and "A Container is an input".

"In-process" means the real app against the fake Docker, or `EngineDockerClient` against a fake Engine on a real port. These probes don't depend on the configuration: nothing here is filtered by the socket proxy, which passes Engine answers through whole.

**By arithmetic, never by load.** Every large figure below is worked out; each is shown once at a small size: a 1 MiB line, an 8 MiB body, 16 MiB of stream events, at most 20 parallel requests. Timings are from the Tester's machine (Apple silicon) and are slower on a small Host.

## Candidate findings

Placeholder IDs until filed.

### #115 (F-X1-1): A log line with no newline is held whole, per stream

- **Severity: medium.** A Container on the Host makes Lookout hold up to ≈ 1 GiB per open log stream; a boundary with no defence in depth ("Nothing is left to a collaborator to bound").
- **Rule broken:** PRD, "A Container is an input … nothing from them is … buffered unbounded"; "Nothing is left to a collaborator to bound".
- **Steps:** run a Container that writes a long line with no newline; open its logs in Lookout.
- **Observed:** nothing is sent until the newline; everything is held, re-split on every chunk; a frame header is believed to 4 GiB. **Expected:** at most 64 KiB held; longer lines sent in pieces.
- **Test that would have caught it:** `X1 H9: a log line with no newline › LineSplitter holds at most 64 KiB …` and `› the frame decoder never waits for more than 64 KiB of one frame`.

### #116 (F-X1-2): Engine answers are read whole, whatever their size

- **Severity: medium.** An Image's `LABEL`s or a Container's environment of any size are read, parsed, kept and sent in every `containers` event.
- **Rule broken:** as #115 (F-X1-1).
- **Observed:** an 8 MiB inspect body is read and kept. **Expected:** answers over 4 MiB are refused, and that Container listed without them.
- **Test:** `X1 H9: Engine bodies are read whole › refuses an Engine answer over 4 MiB`.

### #117 (F-X1-3): Masking a long label blocks Lookout's event loop for minutes

- **Severity: medium** (arguably high: no password needed, and Lookout stops answering for as long as the Container exists). Introduced by #91's fix.
- **Rule broken:** PRD, "A Container is an input"; NFR-1/NFR-2.
- **Steps:** run a Container (or pull an Image) with a label of 64 KiB of letters, then `GET /api/containers`.
- **Observed:** `/[a-z][a-z0-9+.-]*:\/\/…/i` in `docker/secrets.ts` backtracks quadratically: 1.6 s per 64 KiB, ≈ 7.7 min per 1 MiB, on every list and stream event. **Expected:** linear time.
- **Test:** `X1 H9: masking a long label blocks the event loop › checking a value for a secret is linear in its length`.

### #118 (F-X1-4): Nothing bounds a session's streams

- **Severity: medium.** Each log stream holds an Engine follow request and its own buffers; with #115 (F-X1-1) the memory multiplies by the streams.
- **Rule broken:** PRD, "Nothing is left to a collaborator to bound"; the limits table's "Streams per session: None".
- **Observed:** 20 log streams and 20 live streams from one cookie, all 200. **Expected:** 429 past 8 per session.
- **Test:** `X1 H9: streams per session › refuses a session more than 8 open streams`.

### #119 (F-X1-5): A live stream nobody reads holds every event

- **Severity: medium.** No attacker needed: a tab on a half-open connection grows Lookout's memory without end.
- **Rule broken:** PRD, "Nothing is left to a collaborator to bound" (the browser and TCP are the limit today).
- **Observed:** 14.3 MiB held for one stalled stream after 16 MiB of events. **Expected:** at most 1 MiB, then the stream is closed.
- **Test:** `X1 H9: a live stream nobody reads › a stalled /api/stream holds at most 1 MiB, then is closed`.

### #120 (F-X1-6): Past 10,000 addresses the sign-in limiter forgets limited ones

- **Severity: low.** Needs over 10,000 addresses; then only the concurrency cap bounds guessing (≈ 250 a second here). Not recorded as accepted.
- **Observed:** a limited address may guess again after 10,000 other addresses fail. **Expected:** a limited address stays limited for its window.
- **Test:** `X1 H3 › a limited address stays limited however many others fail`.

### #121 (F-X1-7): A Hosted Site URL from a label has no length bound

- **Severity: low.** A Hostile Container or Image puts megabytes into every `sites` event (500 × 64 KiB ≈ 31 MiB).
- **Observed:** 64 KiB URLs kept whole. **Expected:** URLs over 2,048 characters skipped and logged.
- **Test:** `X1: Hosted Sites › a Hosted Site URL over 2,048 characters is skipped`.

### #109 (F-X1-8): The folder scan remembers every hard-linked file

- **Severity: low.** Needs millions of hard-linked files on a filesystem UID 1000 can read; ≈ 70 MiB per million.
- **Observed:** one key per hard-linked inode for the whole walk, no cap. **Expected:** a cap (1,000,000), then counted without de-duplicating.
- **Test:** add an `it.fails` with the cap when it is chosen (X1b); today `today_remembers every hard-linked file for the whole walk`.

## Load and reach

No request left the Tester's machine: the in-process probes opened sockets only to the app's own port and to fake Engines on `127.0.0.1`. The largest demonstration was 20 parallel requests (sign-ins from 20 addresses; 20 log streams); the largest values 8 MiB in memory and 16 MiB written to one local socket. The deployment probes (`x1-limits.probe.ts`) are written to the same limits (1 MiB values, 20 streams) and stay inside the throwaway daemon with `--network none`.
